Privacy Policy — Lands of Decay

Effective date: November 25, 2026. Русская версия

What the game stores

Your progress (heroes, items, settings) is stored on your device in the browser's storage. On game portals (for example CrazyGames or Yandex Games) progress may also be saved with the portal's own save system. In Telegram, a copy may be kept in Telegram's cloud storage linked to your account.

What is sent over the network

  • Co-op play. Only when you create or join a group, the game exchanges gameplay data with the other players in your group (hero position, class, level, combat events) through a public third-party message relay server (MQTT).
  • Leaderboard and world feed. If enabled, your hero's name, class, level and in-game achievements are published.
  • Bug reports. Only if you send one yourself: your text, game version, device and browser type, screen size, place in the game, recent technical errors and, if you choose, a screenshot of the game.

The game does not ask for or collect your real name, phone number, e-mail, location, contacts or payment details.

Play statistics

On some sites the game may use Yandex Metrica to count anonymous visits and game events (source of the visit, device, play time, class chosen, levels and zones reached). It is not used on CrazyGames. Where the game asks for permission, statistics are sent only if you allow them, and they can be turned off in the game settings ("📊 Statistics" button). Processing is governed by Yandex's privacy policy.

Purchases

Where purchases are available, payment goes only through the platform's own system. The game never sees or stores your payment details.

Advertising

Some portals show ads through their own systems, including optional rewarded videos. Data processing for these ads is governed by the privacy policy of the respective portal.

Children

The game does not collect personal data, including from children.

Deleting your data

To delete your progress, clear the site data in your browser settings or delete your hero in the game. You can ask the developer to delete a bug report you sent.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the current version is always on this page.
