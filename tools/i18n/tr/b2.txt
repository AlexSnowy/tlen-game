Controls
Phone: touch the screen and drag — the hero walks and auto-attacks the nearest enemies. Q/E/R — skills, blue button — dash in the movement direction, red — flask. PC: WASD or mouse, Q/E/R, space, F.
Camp
Vera the Merchant buys and sells items and orbs. Grom the Smith enhances items up to +10 and adds stats at the workbench. The stash is a shared chest for all heroes. The portal to the north leads to the lands and maps.
Skill gems
Skills are gems. They drop from monsters (more often from rares and guardians), and the first guardian killed in each zone grants a new one. Socket a gem into equipped gear — the skill appears on the Q/E/R button. Support gems in the same item boost its skills. Socketed gems level up with the hero. Change the item — the gems move to the new one. Grom the Smith adds sockets.
The “To camp” button leaves the portal open: the portal window will show “Return” — the zone stays as you left it. You can also return after death.
The brighter the label and the taller the light beam, the more valuable the item: white — normal, blue — magic, yellow — rare, orange — unique.
better than equipped. The loot filter (button in the bag) hides junk and turns it into gold; you can choose strictness, rarity and item types, and what to pick up automatically.
Transmutation: normal
magic. Alteration: reroll a magic item. Augmentation: +stat to a magic item. Regal: magic
rare. Chaos: reroll a rare item. Exalted: +stat to a rare item. Divine: reroll values. Scouring: remove everything. Vaal: corrupt the item — it may become stronger (enhancement up to +12, special stat) or stay the same, but it can't be changed afterwards. Orbs also work on maps.
Enhancement
Each level gives +10% to all stats of the item. At +1 the weapon shimmers, at +6 an aura appears, at +10 — rays. On failure the gold is lost, the item remains.
Skills and gems
Each class has 21 skills in three branches, three can be equipped. Each skill can hold 2 support gems: more projectiles, chain, area, damage and more. Gems drop from rare monsters and guardians.
Defeating the guardians of the “Cultist Camp”, the “Sunken Ruins”, the “Heart of Decay” and the first tier 5 map grants Ascendancy points. Choose one of two subclasses in the “Passives” window.
Pantheon
Defeating the guardians of the “Ashen Forest” and the “Sunken Ruins” unlocks Pantheon powers (the “Passives” window): one major and one minor. You can change them any time.
Atlas
After the campaign, maps open up: tiers 1–12, rarity and modifiers. More modifiers — more loot. Each conquered tier gives +3% item quantity. Guardians of tier 4+ maps drop Fragments of Decay — 4 fragments open the Lair of the Devourer.
Divination cards
Rare cards from monsters. Collect a full set — Vera the Merchant (“Buy” tab) will trade it for a unique item, orbs, gems or a high-tier map.
Essences
Sometimes a zone has an essence crystal. Come closer — a strong guardian emerges from it. It drops an essence: at the workbench it adds a powerful stat to an item (30% stronger than the best roll), and makes a normal item rare.
Abyss
After the campaign the portal offers the Abyss — an endless descent. The guardian's portal leads to the next depth, monsters and modifiers grow. Every 5 depths is a checkpoint. You can leave with the “To camp” button.
Legion
The Legion monolith freezes a squad of enemies for 10 seconds. Each statue broken before they awaken gives extra loot.
Events
Rift: come closer and the circle fills with creatures — kill inside to extend the time, treasure at the end. Shrines give a strong blessing for 25 seconds.
Party
Up to 3 players. The “Party” button
Create party” and send the link to a friend, or check “Open party” — then everyone will see you in the “Open parties” list. Everyone has their own loot, items can be given to an ally from the bag.
